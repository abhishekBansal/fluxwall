Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 24, 2023  ·  Last Updated: March 30, 2026

Summary: FluxWall (com.rrapps.infinitetunnel) is built with a 100% privacy-first design. We do not collect, process, track, store, or share any personal data or device identifier. Everything runs locally on your device.

1. Application Overview

This Privacy Policy applies to the mobile application FluxWall - Live Wallpapers (package name: com.rrapps.infinitetunnel), developed and published by Abhishek Bansal (operating as Anvik Studio).

Our commitment to privacy is simple: FluxWall provides customizable, real-time live wallpaper animations without gathering any personal information or telemetry from users.

2. Data Collection and Usage

No Personal Data Collection: FluxWall does not collect any personally identifiable information (PII) such as your name, email address, phone number, physical address, location data, or device identifiers (such as IMEI, Android ID, or Advertising ID).

No Telemetry or Analytics: The app does not embed third-party analytics SDKs, error reporting frameworks, or tracking scripts. We do not monitor how often you open the app or which wallpaper presets you select.

3. Storage of Custom Settings

FluxWall allows you to customize wallpaper parameters (such as motion speed, color palettes, scale, and glow intensity) and save your favorite configurations. All saved presets and customization choices are stored exclusively on your local device storage using standard Android storage primitives.

These settings never leave your device and are cleared automatically if you clear the application data or uninstall the app.

4. Advertising and Third Parties

FluxWall is completely ad-free. The application contains no banner ads, interstitial ads, video rewards, or ad networks. Consequently, no ad-tech providers or third parties receive any network requests or data from your device through FluxWall.

5. Device Permissions

FluxWall requests only the minimum permissions necessary to function as an Android Live Wallpaper provider:

  • SET_WALLPAPER / BIND_WALLPAPER: Required by Android operating system to register FluxWall as a active live wallpaper service.

FluxWall does not request permissions for location, contacts, storage access, camera, microphone, or network access.

6. Children's Privacy

FluxWall is suitable for general audiences and rated 3+ on Google Play. Because our application does not collect any personal data whatsoever, FluxWall complies fully with the Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), and Google Play's Family Policy requirements regarding children's privacy.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect functional updates or regulatory requirements. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last Updated" revision date.

Legal & Transparency

Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 20, 2026  ·  Last Updated: November 22, 2026

Summary: FluxWall (com.rrapps.infinitetunnel) is designed to collect as little as possible. There is no account, and the app never asks for your name, email, contacts, or location. To keep the app stable and to improve it, FluxWall collects limited usage and app-performance data through Google's Firebase services. The app works fully without an internet connection; a connection is used only to send this data.

1. Application Overview

This Privacy Policy applies to the mobile application FluxWall - Live Wallpapers (package name: com.rrapps.infinitetunnel), developed and published by Abhishek Bansal (operating as Anvik Studio).

FluxWall provides customizable, real-time live wallpaper animations. We collect only the data we need to keep the app working, fix problems, and understand which features people use so we can improve them.

2. What We Collect

FluxWall uses two Google Firebase services. We do not collect personal details such as your name, email address, phone number, address, contacts, photos, or precise location, because the app has no account and does not ask for them.

  • App performance and crash data (Firebase Crashlytics): crash reports and diagnostics, such as the device model, Android version, app version, and the state of the app when a problem occurred.
  • Usage data (Firebase Analytics): how the app is used, such as app opens, sessions, screens and wallpapers viewed, and basic device and app information (device model, Android version, language, app version, and an approximate region such as country, which Google derives when the data is sent).
  • A random installation identifier: a randomly generated ID created for each installation so that crash and usage data can be counted correctly. It is not linked to your name, email, or any account, and it is not shared with advertisers.

3. How the Data Is Used and Shared

We use this data only to fix crashes, measure app performance, and understand which features are used so we can improve FluxWall. We do not sell it, we do not use it to identify you, and we do not share it with advertisers.

The data is processed for us by Google as a service provider under Google's terms. You can read how Firebase handles data at firebase.google.com/support/privacy.

4. Internet Access

FluxWall does not need an internet connection to work. Every wallpaper, the customization controls, and your saved presets work offline. When a connection is available, the app may send the diagnostics and usage data described above. You can block FluxWall's network access in your device settings (on devices that allow it) and the app will keep working; uninstalling the app stops all collection.

5. Storage of Custom Settings

FluxWall allows you to customize wallpaper parameters (such as motion speed, color palettes, scale, and glow intensity) and save your favorite configurations. All saved presets and customization choices are stored on your device using standard Android storage and are not uploaded by us.

These settings are cleared automatically if you clear the application data or uninstall the app.

6. In-App Purchases and Subscriptions

FluxWall offers optional premium features, including FluxWall Pro subscriptions and individual wallpaper unlocks. All payments and subscriptions are processed directly through Google Play under Google's terms and privacy policies. FluxWall never receives, accesses, or stores your credit card, bank account, or other financial details.

Purchase entitlements are verified through Google Play Billing and stored locally on your device in standard application storage so your unlocked wallpapers work offline.

7. Advertising and Third Parties

FluxWall currently shows no ads. Apart from Google's Firebase services and Google Play Billing described above, no other third party receives data from FluxWall.

8. Device Permissions

FluxWall requests only the permissions it needs:

  • com.android.vending.BILLING: used to process in-app purchases and subscriptions securely through Google Play.
  • BIND_WALLPAPER: a system permission that lets Android run FluxWall as a live wallpaper service.
  • INTERNET and ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE: used only to send the diagnostics and usage data described above when a connection is available. The app does not need them to display wallpapers.

FluxWall does not request permissions for location, contacts, storage access, camera, or microphone.

9. Children's Privacy

FluxWall is a general-audience app. It has no accounts and does not ask for personal information, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, please contact us and we will look into it.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes to the app or legal requirements. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date.

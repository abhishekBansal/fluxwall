Free live wallpaper app, no ads

FluxWall is a free live wallpaper app for Android. Every wallpaper is fully customizable, so your screen can be uniquely yours, and there are no ads and no account to create.

What you get for free

  • Free to download from Google Play.
  • No ads inside the app.
  • No account or sign-up needed. Install it and pick a wallpaper.
  • Moving wallpapers that are drawn live on your phone, not video loops.

Customize every wallpaper

Each wallpaper has its own controls. Depending on the wallpaper, you can adjust color, speed, glow and movement until it feels right. When you find a combination you love, save it as a preset and return to it anytime. Favorites and presets are stored on your device.

Easy on your phone

FluxWall stops drawing when the wallpaper is not visible and caps animation by default. See the details on the battery page.

Frequently asked

Is it really free? Yes. FluxWall is free to download from Google Play.

Do I need an account? No. There is nothing to sign up for.

Are the wallpapers videos? No. They are drawn live on your phone, so they are not fixed loops.

Can I change how a wallpaper looks? Yes. Every wallpaper has controls, and your settings can be saved as presets.

Who it is for

FluxWall works on Android devices that support live wallpapers. If you want a home screen that moves, feels fresh and can be changed with your mood, it is a simple place to start. You can also try the preview editor in your browser first.

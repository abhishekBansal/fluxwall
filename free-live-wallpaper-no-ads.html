Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know
about FluxWall being free.

Is FluxWall really 100% free with no ads? +

Yes. FluxWall is free to download from Google Play. There are zero banner ads, zero interstitial video popups, and zero hidden paywalls inside the app. You get the full app experience without ever being asked to watch an advertisement.

Why are there no ads inside FluxWall? +

We built FluxWall because we believe your phone's home screen should be a calm, beautiful space that reflects your style. Advertising SDKs bloat app downloads, drain battery power in the background, and disrupt your creative flow with frustrating timers. We prefer building software people genuinely enjoy using.

Do I need to register or create an account? +

No. FluxWall does not require an email address, phone number, or social login. You install the app from Google Play and immediately start exploring wallpapers. Your presets, favorites, and settings are saved locally on your phone.

Are any wallpaper worlds or customization controls locked? +

No. Every single one of the 29+ live wallpaper worlds and all customization controls (colors, speed, particles, glow, and motion) are unlocked for all users. You can also preview several featured worlds right in your browser using our interactive studio.

Does FluxWall require an active internet connection? +

No. All FluxWall wallpapers are procedural shader programs that run locally on your phone's GPU. They do not stream video files from remote servers, which means FluxWall works completely offline with zero mobile data consumption.

Will it drain my phone's battery? +

FluxWall is designed with smart lifecycle listeners. When your phone screen turns off or when you open another app, FluxWall stops drawing entirely. Read our detailed technical breakdown on our battery guide.

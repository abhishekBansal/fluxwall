The short answer

What is
FluxWall?

FluxWall is a procedural live wallpaper app for Android. It turns OpenGL shaders into continuously evolving wallpaper scenes that you can preview and customize.

Realtime, not recorded

FluxWall renders each scene on your device instead of playing a fixed video file. The result is motion that can change over time without repeating the same clip.

Made for your screen

Choose a mood, then adjust settings such as color, speed, glow, density, and flow where a world supports them. Save favorites and presets locally.

Android wallpaper studio

FluxWall is available on Google Play for Android devices that support live wallpapers. It does not require an account and does not show ads inside the app.

Good to know

Questions,
answered.

Is it a video or a live effect?+

It is rendered in realtime with OpenGL shaders on your device. The motion is generated continuously, so it never loops through a fixed recording.

Will it drain my battery?+

FluxWall is designed to be efficient. Android pauses the renderer when the screen is off, and the app supports device-friendly frame-rate and detail settings.

Can I use it on the lock screen?+

Yes, on Android versions and launchers that support lock-screen live wallpapers. The wallpaper picker will show the options available on your device.

Does the app have ads?+

No ads, no account, and no tracking inside the app. Your favorites and presets stay on your device.

What can I customize?+

Every world has its own controls: color palettes, speed, glow, density, flow mode, texture, and more. Start with a preset, then tune from there.

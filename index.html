Good to know

Questions,
answered.

Is it a video or a live effect?+

FluxWall creates a moving scene on your phone. It is not a fixed video loop, so the screen can feel alive every time you look at it.

Will it drain my battery?+

FluxWall is designed to be efficient. Android pauses the app when the screen is off, and you can choose device-friendly settings for motion and detail.

Can I use it on the lock screen?+

Yes, on Android versions and launchers that support lock-screen live wallpapers. The wallpaper picker will show the options available on your device.

Does the app have ads?+

No ads, no account, and no tracking inside the app. Your favorites and presets stay on your device.

What can I customize?+

Every world has its own controls for color, speed, glow, density, and more. Start with a preset, then make it feel like yours.
